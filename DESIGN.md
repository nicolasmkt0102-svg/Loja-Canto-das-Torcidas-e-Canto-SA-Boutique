## Brand & Style

The design system bridges two complementary identities under one roof in Cáceres-MT: the refined, feminine curation of a lifestyle boutique alongside the high-energy, passionate universe of sports apparel. The visual language balances warmth, hospitality, and modern sophistication with accessible retail energy.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Tactile Warmth & Soft Minimalism:** Generous negative space, tinted ivory grounds, and organic soft forms prevent clutter, evoking an inviting, physical storefront experience.
- **Duality in Harmony:** Playfair Display introduces editorial elegance and delicate curation, while DM Sans delivers punchy athletic confidence and high legibility across all touchpoints.
- **Conversion-Driven Touch Targets:** Optimized for mobile-first shoppers with prominent, thumb-friendly WhatsApp CTA interactions designed for swift consultative commerce.

## Colors

The palette revolves around deep mineral reds, blush underpinnings, and sunlit chalk surfaces.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#B5474C`):** Direct-action anchors, primary WhatsApp triggers, promotional chips, and active interactive states.
- **Secondary / Deep Burgundy (`#8C3035`):** Structural authority, statement banners, footer backgrounds, and decisive title emphasis.
- **Tertiary / Soft Rose (`#F6E7E4`):** Supportive cards, badges, subtle product frame backdrops, and active toggle containers.
- **Canvas Base / Warm White (`#FBF6F3`):** Global page background delivering natural warmth without sterile starkness.
- **Neutral Dark (`#3B2B2C`):** High-contrast, warm espresso tone for typographic hierarchy and clear legibility.
- **Border Subtle:** `#3B2B2C` rendered at `8%` to `12%` opacity, preventing rigid hard edges while framing content cleanly.

## Typography

Typography establishes distinct customer journeys across boutique and sports segments while preserving systemic cohesion.

- **Boutique & Institutional Headings:** Set in *Playfair Display* (SemiBold 600) for hero lines, collection showcases, and editorial storytelling.
- **Torcida & Fan Apparel Headings:** Set in *DM Sans* (ExtraBold 800) with tight tracking (`-0.03em`) for high-impact matchday promotions and jersey category headers.
- **Body & Operational Labels:** Unified in *DM Sans* across all regular narrative copy, specifications, and button labels to guarantee fast optical scanning on mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

A responsive fluid grid prioritizes hand-held shopping ease and relaxed vertical pacing.

### Screen Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1.25rem` margin, full-bleed horizontal carousels for lookbooks, and sticky bottom navigation elements.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` margin, dual-column product arrangements.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at `1280px` centered canvas, `3rem` outer margin, generous cross-category staging.

### Spacing Philosophy
Content blocks use open vertical rhythms (`space-xl` between modular sections) to recreate the breezy, calm atmosphere of a curated boutique interior.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial layering relies on delicate tonal stepping combined with ultra-diffuse, warm shadows rather than sharp boundaries.

- **Base Layer:** Natural Canvas (`#FBF6F3`).
- **Elevated Surfaces (Cards, Modals):** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) or Secondary Blush (`#F6E7E4`).
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - *Soft Lift (Cards & Product Tiles):* `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.05)`
  - *Floating Focus (Floating WhatsApp Buttons & Drawers):* `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(181, 71, 76, 0.20)`
- **Outlines:** Low-contrast borders (`1px solid rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.08)`) frame white surface cards against the warm ivory ground without adding visual noise.

## Shapes

The shape system employs generous curvature to reinforce a friendly, tactile, and hospitable boutique aesthetic.

- **Pill Geometry:** Utilized across all interactive buttons, status chips, search bars, and floating controls (`rounded-full`).
- **Surface Enclosures:** Category banners, lookbook cards, and dialogue panels strictly employ expansive corner rounding (`1.5rem` to `2rem` / `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl`).
- **Thumbnails & Product Imagery:** Styled with `1rem` to `1.25rem` radius to preserve a soft, organic visual frame.

## Components

### WhatsApp Direct CTAs
- **Primary WhatsApp Trigger:** Large pill button, minimum height `56px`, background `#B5474C`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `label-lg`. Elevated with a subtle warm drop shadow (`0 8px 20px rgba(181, 71, 76, 0.25)`). Accompanied by the WhatsApp icon on the left edge.
- **Floating Concierge CTA:** Fixed bottom-right round/pill button on mobile screens with quick-action greeting triggers.

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Secondary Action:** Pill container, background `#F6E7E4`, text `#8C3035`, font `label-md`. Active states deepen slightly to `#ECCEC8`.
- **Ghost Action:** Border `1px solid rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.15)`, text `#3B2B2C`, no background fill.

### Category Chips & Badges
- **Collection Badges:** Pill-shaped tags with `0.375rem` vertical and `0.875rem` horizontal padding.
- **Active State:** `#8C3035` background with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Inactive State:** `#FFFFFF` background, `#3B2B2C` text, bordered by `rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.08)`.

### Cards & Showcases
- **Product Tiles:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `1.5rem`, internal padding `1rem`. Contained image sits on a tinted `#FBF6F3` or `#F6E7E4` inset canvas.
- **Hero Category Splitter:** Highlights dual store offerings ("Boutique Canto SA" vs. "Canto das Torcidas") using oversized rounded cards (`2rem` radius) with distinctive typographic treatments.

### Form Inputs & Selection Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `52px`, rounded pill silhouette, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.12)`, placeholder text muted in `rgba(59, 43, 44, 0.5)`. Focus transitions to a `2px` ring in `#B5474C` with zero offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded circular elements with `#B5474C` filled selection marks and `#F6E7E4` muted base states.
